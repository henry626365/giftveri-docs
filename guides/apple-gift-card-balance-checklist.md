# Apple gift card balance: a result-recording checklist

Use this checklist to prepare an Apple balance inquiry and retain what it actually establishes. It is a working reference for authorized card checks, not a form for submitting card secrets.

## 1. Define the record you need

Write down one objective before opening the client:

- Inspect the remaining value of an unredeemed Apple card.
- Inspect credit already added to your own Apple Account.
- Understand a message from a previous operation.

Do not combine these objectives into a single “Apple balance” field. They use different evidence. Apple's [gift card balance help](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108111) distinguishes card value from credit already redeemed to an account.

If your objective changes, record the change. A deliberate decision to redeem a card is different from continuing an unsuccessful balance inquiry.

## 2. Prepare the product and country fields

Use a private reference label for the card. Add the full product name and purchase country from the original card or purchase record. Use the [Apple card identification checklist](apple-gift-card-identification.md) when the product is unclear.

An adequate preparation record could say “Card A, product confirmed, purchase country confirmed, card inquiry intended.” A weak record says only “Apple, dollars.” The latter leaves several assumptions unresolved before a request starts.

Keep sensitive card data in the private system where it belongs. A public issue needs the product and region, not the complete code or an unredacted photo.

## 3. Choose the correct official route

For an unredeemed card, begin with Apple's card-balance resource above. For account credit, use Apple's [account balance instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119902) for your device and the intended account.

Account credit should be recorded as account credit. It is not the total of all unused cards you possess. Likewise, a card inquiry does not establish which account was used for a separate purchase.

When following a help article, read the operation it describes before acting. An instruction about redemption is not permission to redeem a card simply to collect diagnostic information.

## 4. Check the actual GiftVeri workflow

Consult the [GiftVeri Apple US scope and verification record](https://www.giftveri.com/en/brands/apple/us) before selecting that entry. The published US record does not claim fully confirmed balance reads. A request path, plan entitlement or initialized session is insufficient evidence of a returned amount.

Use the [Windows setup guide](../docs/getting-started.md) for installation. Keep version, selected regional entry, session readiness and task result distinguishable. Prepare one authorized card before considering a larger list.

If a readiness step fails, record that stage. Avoid attaching a business label such as invalid or inactive unless the relevant response actually supplies it.

## 5. Preserve the result without filling gaps

The examples below are invented documentation examples.

| Private label | Operation | Recorded outcome | Open question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Card A | Card balance inquiry | Explicit amount and currency | No conclusion about prior transactions |
| Card B | Card balance inquiry | Amount absent after a connection problem | Balance and card status remain unknown |
| Account C | Account balance view | Account credit displayed | No conclusion about another unredeemed card |

Record the response before interpreting it. Keep amount, currency, operation and message in separate fields where possible. A note saying that a request finished should never silently turn into a zero amount in an export.

For a precise reading policy, use [gift card balance results](../docs/results.md). If you annotate the result later, keep the original response meaning visible so a reviewer can distinguish observation from your explanation.

## 6. Decide whether another request is appropriate

Separate tasks that were not sent from tasks with an uncertain outcome. A missing amount alone is insufficient reason to send the same task again. Review the stage, session and intended operation before deciding.

If country evidence conflicts, use [Apple region preflight](apple-gift-card-region-preflight.md). If a specific error was returned, use [Apple message triage](apple-gift-card-message-triage.md). Choose the next guide from the evidence, rather than cycling through every checker available.

## Completion criteria

A useful record identifies the card or account objective, product, country evidence, operation and observed outcome. Unknown fields remain unknown. Any public report is redacted, and the next action follows the observed issue rather than an assumed balance.
