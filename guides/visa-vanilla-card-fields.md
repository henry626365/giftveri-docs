# Visa and Vanilla gift card balance: matching card fields

Visa is a payment network. The issuer and exact product determine where and how a balance inquiry is made.

## Choose an issuer route

Begin with the instructions on the card or its original digital delivery. The [Visa gift card balance guide](https://www.visa.com/en-us/personal/cards/gift/gift-card-balance) points to several issuer services, including Vanilla. That does not make every Visa-branded card a Vanilla product.

Use [Vanilla Gift's official help](https://www.vanillagift.com/help) for the Vanilla Gift product it covers. Confirm the product and region before entering any information.

## Match each field to its purpose

- **Card number:** use the number for the prepaid card itself, rather than a packaging or order reference.
- **Expiration:** match the physical card or official eGift view.
- **Security code:** follow the issuer's label; do not substitute a retailer's access number.
- **PIN:** use the instructions for the operation that requires it. Do not assume every inquiry uses a payment PIN.

Stop if the requested fields do not match the product. Rewriting a field to pass a form does not establish that the checker is correct.

## Scope of the GiftVeri entry

The [GiftVeri Vanilla US page](https://www.giftveri.com/en/brands/vanilla/us) describes prerequisites and a standalone single-card development workflow. Check its current release and verification notes before use.

It is not a claim of stable-release availability, universal Visa support or Vanilla batch support. Confirm activation and authorization through the existing records the workflow requires; do not use the development flow to probe an unclear activation state.

## Retain an uncertain outcome

A failed inquiry cannot be converted to a zero balance. A returned balance also does not guarantee that a purchase will be accepted. Keep the operation and response distinct, following [result interpretation](../docs/results.md).

For the general product-identification process, use the [balance-check checklist](gift-card-balance-checklist.md).
