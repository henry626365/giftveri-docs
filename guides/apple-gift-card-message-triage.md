# Apple gift card not working: triage the message and operation

Use this decision record to preserve what an Apple card problem actually says. A single error label should not erase whether the problem arose during a balance inquiry, account inspection, redemption or client setup.

## Start with the operation

Write the action first, followed by the message. For example, “card inquiry; no amount after a connection problem” is more precise than “bad card.” The first description tells another person what evidence exists and what still needs investigation.

Apple's [redemption troubleshooting](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108285) describes several card messages. Use its matching help section when that is the operation involved. A third-party client's session error does not become an Apple redemption decision merely because the same card was nearby.

## Route the observation

| Observation | Next investigation | Keep unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product or code rejected | Identify the product and requested field | Any balance not actually returned |
| Activation message | Retain receipt and follow retailer guidance | The outcome of the retailer's review |
| Prior-redemption message | Inspect relevant account records and official help | An account you have not identified |
| Country message | Compare purchase country and storefront | Any amount not explicitly established |
| Code unreadable | Follow the official damaged-code process | The missing characters |
| Session or connection error | Locate the client stage and submission state | Card value and business status |

These routes are investigation choices. They do not promise activation, replacement, recovery or a successful balance response.

## Preserve field and product evidence

Use [Apple product identification](apple-gift-card-identification.md) when an old store-only card may have entered an App Store workflow. Describe the product wording and form label. Do not copy the code into a public issue to ask which field it belongs in.

Keep the serial number, redeemable code and purchase reference distinct. A string accepted by a field is not proof of correct product selection. For damaged information, preserve the original and use verified support instead of fabricating alternatives.

## Inspect an account only when that is the relevant record

If the card was redeemed to your own account, follow Apple's [account balance help](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119902). Keep the account amount separate from a card amount and from any subsequent purchase history.

Do not infer the identity of an account from an error alone. If an account cannot be identified from your own legitimate records, leave that question unresolved and use the applicable support process.

If redemption was never intended, do not perform it as an experiment. An action that moves value is different from a read-only attempt to understand an amount.

## Locate the failed GiftVeri stage

Use this order when describing a client failure:

1. The application and version could be opened.
2. Platform access was or was not available.
3. The relevant Apple entry and session were or were not ready.
4. The card task was unsent, sent or uncertain.
5. The response did or did not establish an amount or specific business outcome.

Stop at the first unresolved step. Later steps cannot be claimed merely because an earlier one succeeded. In particular, a ready session does not establish a returned balance.

The [GiftVeri US Apple verification record](https://www.giftveri.com/en/brands/apple/us) retains that distinction. Use [reading results](../docs/results.md) and [client troubleshooting](../docs/troubleshooting.md) for operational detail.

## Decide on the next attempt explicitly

Keep an uncertain task separate from a clearly unsent one. Review its state before any retry, especially after changing a session or region. Use [Apple region preflight](apple-gift-card-region-preflight.md) when regional evidence is missing or conflicting.

A useful next-action note identifies what will be checked and why. “Review the submission state before retrying” preserves an open question. “Run again until a number appears” does not provide a reliable interpretation rule.

## Share a redacted report

Include product, purchase country, operation, time, client version where relevant and the stage that failed. Preserve the precise meaning of the message while removing private values. Keep codes, passwords, verification codes, account credentials and session data out of public reports.

The record is complete when its observation, unresolved questions and next action are clear. Completion of the record does not mean that the card issue itself has been resolved.
