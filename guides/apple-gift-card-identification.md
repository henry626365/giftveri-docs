# Apple Gift Card, App Store and Apple Store: identify the product

Complete product identification before selecting a balance or redemption workflow. This checklist helps organize the evidence without exposing a code or treating a design resemblance as proof.

## Create three independent fields

Use separate fields for the product name, purchase country and intended operation. A single label such as “Apple US card” can conceal that only one of those details was established.

For each field, record the evidence privately. This could be wording on the original card, an original delivery email or a purchase record. If the evidence is missing, use an unknown value rather than choosing a likely answer from a search image.

Apple's [gift card identification resource](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118407) covers current and older products. Its examples help identify a generation; they do not replace reading the actual card.

## Compare the product wording

| Evidence on the product | Identification task | Workflow question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Gift Card | Confirm that exact name and purchase country | Are you inspecting a card or account credit? |
| App Store or App Store & iTunes | Retain the legacy name in the record | Which current instructions apply to this product? |
| Apple Store Gift Card | Read any store-only purchase wording | Are you mistakenly using an App Store route? |
| A different older design | Preserve its own instructions | Which official help describes that generation? |

An older name does not by itself establish an invalid code. A card that resembles a newer design does not establish that it has the newer product's purchase scope. Resolve explicit wording before interpreting a rejection.

## Keep purchase scope separate from balance

Use Apple's [eligible purchases guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118245) for what the relevant card or account credit can buy. A statement about permitted purchases is different from evidence that the card has a particular amount.

For older US store-only cards, the [US payment help](https://www.apple.com/shop/help/payments) supplies the specific distinction from account credit. Apply the instructions to the product and country described by the source.

A useful support note might say “store-only wording confirmed; account redemption had been selected.” That identifies a mismatch without claiming that the balance is zero or that the card is defective.

## Match the field to the operation

Read the requested label before entering a value. Keep the card's redeemable code, serial number and the seller's order reference distinct. Similar length or placement on the card does not make one a valid replacement for another.

When the objective is redemption, use Apple's [redemption instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118242) for that device and product. When the objective is inspection, use the [Apple balance checklist](apple-gift-card-balance-checklist.md) instead.

For documentation or a public issue, describe the label and product without copying the complete value. A fake placeholder should be clearly labeled as invented; it should not resemble a live input that someone might submit.

## Stop when the evidence conflicts

Do not resolve a mismatch by repeatedly changing the product name in an application. Keep the original label and investigate the conflicting evidence first. A form accepting a string establishes only that the form accepted it, not that the correct card workflow was chosen.

If the code cannot be read, follow the applicable official support process and retain the card and receipt privately. Do not reconstruct a missing code as part of product identification.

If the product is known but the country is not, move to [region preflight](apple-gift-card-region-preflight.md). Do not let a confirmed product name erase an unresolved purchase-country field.

## Match the GiftVeri entry last

The [GiftVeri selection guide](https://www.giftveri.com/en/guides/choose-brand-card-region) explains the client-side choice. Use it after the product and country checks. A menu label does not establish support for every older Apple product.

Record the entry actually selected, then inspect one authorized result. Keep a failed session or missing amount distinct from a product decision. Product identification is complete when its evidence is clear; a balance result remains a separate acceptance step.
